VICTIM LOG: The First Casualties

CASE #1: MARY KELLERMAN
Age: Twelve Years Old
Time of Death: 07:00 Hours

Mary complained of a mild cold and
sore throat to her parents. She took
one Extra-Strength Tylenol capsule.

She collapsed on the bathroom floor
moments later. Paramedics could not
revive her. She was the first known
victim of the Chicago Tylenol Murders.

---

CASE #2: ADAM JANUS
Age: Twenty-Seven Years Old
Time of Death: 12:00 Hours

A postal worker, Adam stopped to buy
medicine for minor chest pain.

He collapsed in his bedroom. Initially,
doctors suspected a massive cardiac
event due to his sudden death. His
family gathered at his home to mourn.

---

CASE #3: STANLEY JANUS
Age: Twenty-Five Years Old
Time of Death: 17:00 Hours

Stanley, Adam's younger brother,
developed a headache while at Adam's
house grieving.

He found the open bottle of Tylenol on
the kitchen counter—the same bottle
that killed Adam. He offered one to his
wife, Theresa.

Both collapsed simultaneously in front
of family members.
