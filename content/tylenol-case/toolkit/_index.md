EVIDENCE LOG: Physical Apparatus

ITEM #1: THE DELIVERY MECHANISM
Object: Extra-Strength Tylenol Red Capsules
Configuration: Two-piece gelatin shell
Status: Compromised

The perpetrator utilized a method of
mechanical separation to open the
capsules.

Forensic analysis indicates:
- No gross deformation of the capsule shell
- No tearing of the gelatin material
- Skilled manipulation required to open
  without leaving obvious tool marks

The substance introduced was potassium
cyanide, a fine white powder, which
blended visually with the white
acetaminophen powder.

---

ITEM #2: THE RE-SEALING PROCESS
Object: Bottle assembly and adhesive
Observation: Microscopic residue

Investigators noted that the bottles
were re-glued with high precision.

Technique required:
- Removal of the glued paper safety flap
- Introduction of the tainted capsules
- Application of fresh adhesive
- Re-application of the flap to mimic
  factory standards

This suggests the individual had:
- Access to a workspace
- Time to perform the delicate operation
- Patience to wait for adhesive drying
  before returning items to shelves

---

ITEM #3: DISTRIBUTION TOOLS
Object: Vehicle and Placement
Status: Unknown

The logistical toolkit required a vehicle
to transport the bottles to multiple
locations across the western suburbs.

Locations hit included:
- Arlington Heights
- Elk Grove Village
- Schaumburg
- Winfield

The haphazard geographical spread
suggests a random seeding pattern rather
than a targeted attack on specific
consumers.
