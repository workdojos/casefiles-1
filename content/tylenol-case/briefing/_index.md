SITUATION REPORT: Initial Assessment

INCIDENT SUMMARY
Date: September 29, 1982
Casualties: Seven Confirmed
Agent: Cyanide Poisoning

Law enforcement agencies faced a
scenario with no precedent. Random
acts of domestic terrorism via
consumer goods were unknown.

The primary challenge was information
containment and dissemination.

---

OPERATIONAL HURDLES
Issue: Identification
Status: Difficult

There were no visual indicators on the
packaging to warn consumers.

The cyanide kills within minutes of
ingestion. By the time first responders
arrive, the evidence (the pill) has
dissolved in the victim's stomach.

This necessitated the seizure of all
physical evidence from homes, including
trash cans, to find the bottles.

---

COORDINATION EFFORTS
Agencies Involved:
- Elk Grove Village Police
- Arlington Heights Police
- Illinois State Police
- Federal Bureau of Investigation

A multi-jurisdictional task force was
established within forty-eight hours.

Their first directive was to sweep
every drug store in Cook County to
remove products before more purchases
could be made.
