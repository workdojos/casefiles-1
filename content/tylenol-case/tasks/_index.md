TASK FORCE LOG: Procedural Actions

PRIORITY #1: THE RECALL ORDER
Action: Nationwide Withdrawal
Scope: 31 Million Bottles
Cost: 100 Million Dollars

Johnson and Johnson executed an
unprecedented retrieval of all products.

Logistical steps taken:
- Immediate cease of advertising
- Halt of all production lines
- Coordination with the Food and Drug
  Administration
- Offers of replacement tablets for
  all consumers holding capsules

This action is cited as the primary
reason the death toll remained at seven.

---

PRIORITY #2: FORENSIC TESTING
Action: Chemical Analysis
Volume: Eight Million Capsules Tested

The Chicago crime lab and Federal Bureau
of Investigation initiated mass testing.

Methodology:
- Visual inspection of millions of pills
- Chemical sampling of suspect lots
- Coordination with toxicology experts

Results found seventy-five contaminated
capsules across the recovered bottles.
Most bottles had five to seven poisoned
capsules mixed in with the safe ones.

---

PRIORITY #3: SURVEILLANCE
Action: Suspect Tracking
Target: Retail Locations

Agents were deployed to monitor retail
locations in the affected suburbs.

Objectives:
- Identify individuals returning products
- Watch for suspicious shelf behavior
- Interview pharmacists and clerks
- Review security camera footage

Unfortunately, video surveillance in
nineteen eighty-two was rare and low
quality, yielding no visual evidence
of the tamperer stocking the shelves.
