SUSPECT FILES: ONE THROUGH THREE

SUSPECT NUMBER ONE: JAMES WILLIAM LEWIS
Status: Primary suspect, but never charged with murder.
Age at time: Thirty-six years old.
Connection: Extortion letter.

On October fifth, nineteen eighty-two, Johnson and Johnson 
received an extortion letter demanding 
one million dollars to "stop the killing."

The letter was postmarked from New York. 
The writer identified himself and gave 
bank account details for payment.

Lewis was tracked down and arrested. 
He was convicted of extortion in nineteen eighty-three. 
He served thirteen years in federal prison.

CRITICAL NOTE:
He was never charged with the murders. 
There was no physical evidence linking him 
to the tampering.

The Federal Bureau of Investigation investigated him 
for decades. Agents searched his home in Cambridge, 
Massachusetts, in two thousand and nine. 
No charges were filed.

Lewis died in twenty twenty-three. 
The case remains unsolved.

---

SUSPECT NUMBER TWO: ROGER ARNOLD  
Status: Investigated and cleared.
Age at time: Forty-eight years old.
Connection: Accusation from a jealous coworker.

A dock worker at a Jewel Foods warehouse. 
An anonymous tip claimed he was "unstable" 
and had made threats about poisoning.

The tip came from a coworker with a grudge.

Police raided his home. 
He was arrested and held. 
No evidence was found.

Arnold was cleared after the investigation.

He later sued for wrongful arrest. 
The false accusation destroyed 
his life and his reputation.

---

SUSPECT NUMBER THREE: LAURIE DANN
Status: Investigated, and later convicted 
        of a separate mass murder.
Age at time: Twenty-four years old.  
Connection: Similar methods used in later crimes.

Dann was briefly investigated for 
the Tylenol murders due to her 
history of poisoning attempts.

In nineteen eighty-eight, she committed a mass murder 
at an elementary school in Winnetka, Illinois. 
She had previously poisoned food 
and sent poisoned items through the mail.

Investigators noted similarities 
to the Tylenol case methodology.

No evidence was found linking her to nineteen eighty-two. 
She died by suicide after the nineteen eighty-eight attack.

The connection remains speculative.
