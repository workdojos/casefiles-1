SUSPECT FILES: 1-3

SUSPECT #1: JAMES WILLIAM LEWIS
Status: Primary suspect (never charged)
Age at time: 36 years old
Connection: Extortion letter

On October 5, 1982, Johnson & Johnson
received an extortion letter demanding
$1 million to "stop the killing."

Letter postmarked from New York.
Writer identified himself and gave
bank account details for payment.

Lewis was tracked down and arrested.
Convicted of extortion in 1983.
Served 13 years in federal prison.

CRITICAL NOTE:
Never charged with the murders.
No physical evidence linking him
to the tampering.

FBI investigated him for decades.
Searched his home in Cambridge, MA
in 2009. No charges filed.

Lewis died in 2023.
Case remains unsolved.

---

SUSPECT #2: ROGER ARNOLD  
Status: Investigated and cleared
Age at time: 48 years old
Connection: Jealous coworker accusation

Dock worker at Jewel Foods warehouse.
Anonymous tip claimed he was "crazy"
and made threats about poisoning.

Tip came from coworker with grudge.

Police raided his home.
Arrested and held.
No evidence found.

Arnold was CLEARED after investigation.

He later sued for wrongful arrest.
The false accusation destroyed
his life and reputation.

---

SUSPECT #3: LAURIE DANN
Status: Investigated, later convicted
        of separate mass murder
Age at time: 24 years old  
Connection: Similar MO in later crimes

Dann was briefly investigated for
the Tylenol murders due to her
history of poisoning attempts.

In 1988, she committed mass murder
at elementary school in Winnetka, IL.
She had previously poisoned food
and sent poisoned items.

Investigators noted similarities
to Tylenol case methodology.

No evidence linking her to 1982.
She died by suicide after 1988 attack.

Connection remains speculative.
