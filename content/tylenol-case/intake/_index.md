SUBJECT LOG: Persons of Interest

PROFILE #1: JAMES LEWIS
Status: Primary Suspect (Extortion)
Location: New York City

Lewis sent a letter to Johnson and
Johnson demanding one million dollars.

Evidence against him:
- Admitted to writing the extortion letter.
- Provided detailed bank routing numbers.
- Had a history of violent behavior.

However, flight records and credit card
receipts placed him in New York during
the time the bottles were placed on
shelves in Chicago.

He was convicted of extortion, not
the murders.

---

PROFILE #2: ROGER ARNOLD
Status: Person of Interest
Location: Chicago Area

Arnold worked at a Jewel warehouse
distribution center.

Suspicious factors:
- Possessed a chemistry background.
- Owned a manual on killing.
- Admitted to possessing potassium
  cyanide for "projects."

He was cleared after a police breakdown
in procedure, but the attention ruined
his life. He was never charged with
the tampering.

---

PROFILE #3: THE UNABOMBER THEORY
Subject: Ted Kaczynski
Status: Investigated

The FBI analyzed Kaczynski due to his
proximity to Chicago and his parents'
home in Lombard, Illinois.

Similarities noted:
- Targeting random civilians.
- Anti-corporate motivation.
- Skilled creation of devices.

DNA analysis performed years later
on the stamp from the extortion letter
failed to match Kaczynski or Lewis.
