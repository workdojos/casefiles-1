CASE FILE: TYLENOL MURDERS
Chicago Metropolitan Area
September 29 - October 1, 1982

Welcome, Detective.

You've been assigned to review
the Tylenol Murders case file.
This is one of the most significant
product tampering cases in history.

CASE SUMMARY:
Seven victims died after consuming
Extra-Strength Tylenol capsules
laced with potassium cyanide.

Deaths occurred over 3 days.
All victims in Chicago area.
Bottles purchased from different stores.
Tampering occurred after manufacture.

The case remains UNSOLVED.

Where would you like to begin?
