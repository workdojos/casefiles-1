LOCATION LOG: The Affected Areas

ZONE #1: ARLINGTON HEIGHTS
Type: Residential Suburb
Impact: Ground Zero

This quiet community was the site of the
Janus family tragedy.

Three family members died within hours
of each other in the same house.
- Adam Janus died first.
- Stanley and Theresa died while
  mourning Adam.

The local hospital, Northwest Community,
became the command center for the
initial medical response.

---

ZONE #2: ELK GROVE VILLAGE
Type: Commercial and Residential
Impact: The First Victim

Mary Kellerman, age twelve, purchased
her bottle from a Jewel Foods store
located in this municipality.

The close proximity of these suburbs
(less than five miles apart) helped
investigators realize this was a
localized event.

---

ZONE #3: THE RETAIL CORRIDOR
Type: Distribution Points
