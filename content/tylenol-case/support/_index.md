OPERATIONAL LOG: Support & Timeline

SEQUENCE #1: THE INITIAL CHAOS
Date: September 29, 1982
Status: Critical

First responders arrived at multiple
locations with no knowledge of a link.

06:30 AM: Mary Kellerman collapses.
12:00 PM: Adam Janus collapses.
03:15 PM: Stanley and Theresa Janus
collapse at the same location as Adam.

Medical examiners initially suspected:
- Carbon monoxide leak
- Genetic heart defect
- Massive stroke

The connection was not made until
firefighters heard the same brand name
mentioned on radio channels.

---

SEQUENCE #2: THE CONNECTION
Date: September 30, 1982
Status: Confirmed

Nurse Helen Jensen and investigator
Nick Pishos compared evidence.

Key discoveries:
- Both the Kellerman and Janus families
  had open bottles of Tylenol.
- Both bottles had the same control
  number: MC2880.
- The capsules smelled like almonds.

This distinct odor is the hallmark
signature of cyanide gas.

---

SEQUENCE #3: PUBLIC SAFETY
Date: October 1, 1982
Status: Alert Issued

Police drove through the streets of
Chicago using loudspeakers.

The message was clear and urgent:
- Do not take Tylenol capsules.
- Discard any bottles currently in
  your possession.
- Report suspicious symptoms immediately.

Hospitals were flooded with hundreds
of "worried well" patients fearing
they had been poisoned, straining
the medical support system.
